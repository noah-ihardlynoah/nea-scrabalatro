(Balatro with Scrabble tiles)

As a gambling-obsessed teenager with no real money, the poker-based roguelite Balatro has been one of my favourite games since I discovered it last year. However, over the past few months, there have been multiple awkward moments with parents, teachers and other authority figures where they have caught a brief glimpse of the game, immediately panicked and slammed my laptop shut, instinctively thinking that I must be shelling out 100s of pounds to a seedy online gambling website. Hence, I present to you SCRABALATRO - the completely original word-based roguelite which proudly takes no inspiration from any other games like Scrabble or Balatro.
